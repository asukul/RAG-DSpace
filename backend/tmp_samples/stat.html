Alicia L Carriquiry

Pronouns
She/her/hers
Position
  • Distinguished Professor and President's Chair
  • Director of the Center for Statistics and Applications in Forensic Evidence
I am interested in the principled application of statistics to solve substantive problems in disciplines including forensic science, nutrition epidemiology, plant and animal breeding, and others. I gravitate toward the Bayesian framework, but I am not obsessive.
I have had the good fortune of contributing to the profession through a series of leadership and editorial roles and have been generously recognized by multiple organizations.
